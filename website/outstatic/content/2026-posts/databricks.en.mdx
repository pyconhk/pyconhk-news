## Supporting the Python community and the possibilities of data and AI

HONG KONG — PyCon Hong Kong 2026 is honored to welcome **[Databricks](https://www.databricks.com/)** as a sponsor of this year’s conference. We sincerely thank Databricks for supporting our community and helping us create a space for Python enthusiasts, developers, and data and AI practitioners to connect, learn, and exchange ideas.

## Bringing Data, Analytics, and AI Together

Databricks is the data and AI company. Its Data Intelligence Platform brings together data, analytics, and AI on an open lakehouse foundation, helping organizations turn data into insights and build secure applications.

By bringing these capabilities together, Databricks helps organizations put their data to work—from understanding information to developing applications that address real-world needs.

## Rooted in Open Source

Founded by the original creators of Apache Spark™, Delta Lake, MLflow, and Unity Catalog, Databricks is on a mission to simplify and democratize data and AI—helping businesses solve the world’s toughest problems.

This focus on making technology more accessible resonates with the spirit of PyCon Hong Kong: bringing people together to share knowledge, explore ideas, and learn from one another.

We are grateful to Databricks for supporting PyCon Hong Kong 2026 and look forward to welcoming our community for conversations and collaboration across Python, data, and AI.
