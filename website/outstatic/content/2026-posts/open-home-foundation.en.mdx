## Supporting open source, privacy, and greater choice in the connected home

HONG KONG — PyCon Hong Kong 2026 is honored to welcome **[Open Home Foundation](https://www.openhomefoundation.org/)** as a sponsor of this year’s conference. We sincerely thank the foundation for supporting the Python and open-source community and for championing a future in which technology serves the people who use it.

## A Smart Home Built Around People

Smart homes should be private spaces where people can be themselves—not sources of data for Big Tech seeking to monetize personal information. Open Home Foundation offers an alternative: a smart home built on privacy, choice, and sustainability for everyone.

Founded in 2024, Open Home Foundation is a tax-exempt Swiss non-profit that owns and governs more than 250 open-source projects, including the Home Assistant home automation platform.

The foundation is funded by its commercial partners, Nabu Casa and Apollo Automation, as well as independent donations. This structure ensures that its core projects can never be sold, with resources invested in developing and promoting open standards and tools that serve its community of users—not advertisers or shareholders.

## Connecting Open Source with Everyday Life

Beyond its work on open-source projects, Open Home Foundation advocates for the ethical use of smart home technology across the wider industry. It engages companies and the general public on the value of open standards and the vision of the Open Home.

For PyCon Hong Kong 2026, this sponsorship is an opportunity to bring the open-source community and the Open Home vision closer together. We look forward to encouraging conversations about how open technologies can give people greater control over their digital lives while respecting their privacy.

We thank Open Home Foundation for its generous support of PyCon Hong Kong 2026 and for helping strengthen a community built on openness, collaboration, and shared learning.

## About Open Home Foundation

Open Home Foundation is a Swiss non-profit founded in 2024 to advance a smart home built on privacy, choice, and sustainability. It owns and governs more than 250 open-source projects, including Home Assistant. Through open standards, open-source tools, and public advocacy, the foundation works towards an Open Home that serves the interests of its users.
