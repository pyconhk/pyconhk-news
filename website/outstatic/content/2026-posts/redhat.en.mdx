## Supporting open-source collaboration and innovation across the Python community

HONG KONG — PyCon Hong Kong 2026 is honored to welcome **[Red Hat](https://www.redhat.com/)** as a sponsor of this year’s conference. We sincerely thank Red Hat for supporting our community and helping us bring together Python enthusiasts, developers, and technology professionals to learn, share, and collaborate.

## Enabling Innovation Across the Open Hybrid Cloud

Red Hat is the open hybrid cloud technology leader, delivering a trusted, consistent, and comprehensive foundation for transformative IT innovation and AI applications.

Its portfolio spans cloud, developer, AI, Linux, automation, and application platform technologies, enabling any application, anywhere—from the datacenter to the edge. These technologies help organizations address evolving IT needs while building a foundation for future innovation.

## Investing in Open Source and Communities

As the world’s leading provider of enterprise open-source software solutions, Red Hat invests in open ecosystems and communities to solve tomorrow’s IT challenges.

Working with partners and customers, Red Hat helps organizations build, connect, automate, secure, and manage their IT environments. This work is supported by consulting services and award-winning training and certification offerings.

Red Hat’s commitment to open ecosystems and community collaboration resonates with the spirit of PyCon Hong Kong: creating opportunities for people to exchange knowledge, learn from one another, and explore what they can build together.

We are grateful to Red Hat for supporting PyCon Hong Kong 2026 and look forward to bringing our community together for conversations, shared learning, and open-source collaboration.
