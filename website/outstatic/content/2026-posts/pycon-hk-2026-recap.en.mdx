PyCon HK 2026 brought the Python community together at HKIIT in Tseung Kwan O on 14 and 15 November. Thank you to everyone who shared an idea, gave a talk, contributed to a project or helped run the two days.

## Development Sprint results

On Sunday, participants worked together on five Sprint projects. Four teams shared their presentation materials:

- [nsh-py Sprint slides](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VNg6nnTYm7WSm5XWWA3hZLDqKuyYhmau/view)
- [Training an LLM with MiniMind](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1I9auW_lJHE9LMgpmeCDUNdNrYq1HugGy/view)
- [Django Sprint recap](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/17z8ocS76H6CQkF7daQOVdXD3ytAfM3Hw/edit)
- [Video tagging with a decision model](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1R_FulVe5KEfg1KlPfNcikqBosnEEBcAu/view)

The nsh-py project merged 16 pull requests tagged for PyCon HK 2026, including badge and tab components, a fix for Ctrl-C on macOS, and a Kanban example. [Browse the merged pull requests](https://github.com/lucianosrp/nsh-py/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+label%3Apycon-hk-26)

The video tagging team built an annotation tool with questions configurable in YAML and demonstrated the workflow on video frames. [Read the project's results summary](https://github.com/dev1virtuoso/Event/blob/main/HKPUG/PyCon%202026/Sprint/Draft/sprint-summary.pdf)

## [PyShare](/2026/en/pyshare/)

The closing session announced two winning PyShare entries:

- BECOME NVIDIA CEO
- FRUSTRATION USING BLOCKCHAIN

The prizes were a one-month CaLoMei subscription and a one-year JetBrains PyCharm subscription.

## [Job Board](/2026/en/job-board/)

The Job Board gave attendees a place to share job and internship opportunities and meet others looking to hire or find their next role.
